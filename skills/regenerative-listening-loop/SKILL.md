# Regenerative Listening Loop

Turn consultation into a closed loop: listen, understand, act, report back, and learn together.

## Systemic contract

A valid loop shows benefits and indicators in at least three of the human, social, knowledge, resources, and ecology lenses; checks harm in all five; pairs the reinforcing trust loop with safeguards against consultation fatigue and majority dominance; leaves reusable learning or stronger relationships; budgets participant attention, compensation, data, facilitation, and follow-up; and distinguishes evidence from hope.

Before action, name at least one resource or capability expected to end above baseline and set a limit for every material resource. After action, call the outcome regenerative only when indicators support that threshold; a gain in one unit does not erase a deficit in another.

## Workflow

1. State what feedback can change, who owns the response, and when participants will hear back. Stop if nothing can change.
2. Map affected groups and select channels that reduce language, disability, technology, safety, and schedule barriers.
3. Obtain informed consent. Collect only the data needed, define retention, and offer anonymous or non-digital routes when appropriate.
4. Ask open questions about lived experience, assets, needs, risks, and ideas. Do not lead people toward a preferred answer.
5. Synthesize common themes, important exceptions, dissent, and missing voices. Preserve context and avoid fabricated precision.
6. Validate the synthesis with participants or representatives before acting.
7. Publish or deliver a plain-language response: what was heard, what will change, what will not, why, who owns each action, and when it will be reviewed.
8. Track actions and return with results. Ask whether the process itself improved trust and usefulness.

## Output

Create a feedback-loop plan with purpose, influence boundary, participants and gaps, consent and privacy plan, channels, questions, synthesis method, response promise, action register, resource budget, success and harm indicators, review date, and closure message.

Respond in the user's language. Define specialized terms on first use and preserve precise terms, evidence, uncertainty, and caveats in the technical layer.

## Boundaries

Do not call data collection engagement when participants receive no influence or response. Do not expose quotes or identities without consent. Escalate immediate safeguarding risks through appropriate human channels.
