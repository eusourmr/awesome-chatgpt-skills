# Community conduct

We want contributions to be practical, respectful, and evidence-led.

- Discuss the work, not the worth of the person proposing it.
- Give clear reasons for acceptance, requested changes, or rejection.
- Credit original authors and correct attribution mistakes promptly.
- Do not use issues or pull requests for harassment, spam, impersonation, or undisclosed promotion.
- Disclose affiliations when submitting your own project or one you represent.
- Protect personal information and report security concerns without publishing exploitable details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its contributors.
